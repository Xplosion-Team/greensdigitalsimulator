# 🎓 Mirna's Master Training Plan

## A Step-by-Step Guide to Becoming the Digital Twin Architect

**Objective**: To take you from "What is a glucose curve?" to "I built an autonomous AI agent."

This plan is ordered by **importance**. You cannot build the roof (AI Agents) before you pour the foundation (Domain Knowledge).

---

### Phase 1: The Foundation (Domain Knowledge) 🩸 [COMPLETED] 🟢

*Why: You cannot code what you do not understand.*

* **Goal**: Understand why we are doing this. What is "Time in Range"? What happens when you drink coffee?
* **The Module**: [CGM Baseline Training](./modules/cgm-baseline-training.md)
* **Action Item**: Run the "Coffee Experiment" on yourself (or simulate it mentally) to understand the data.

---

### Phase 2: The Blueprint (Architecture) 🏗️ [COMPLETED] 🟢

*Why: Understand the "Headless" design before you write a single line of code.*

* **Goal**: Learn why we split the "brain" (Python) from the "face" (Lovable).
* **The Module**: [API Development Strategy](./modules/api-development-brains.md)
* **Action Item**: Read the "Waiter Analogy" and understand the JSON payload structure.

---

### Phase 3: The Bridge (Integration) 🔌 [COMPLETED] 🟢

*Why: Make the pieces talk to each other.*

* **Goal**: Connect your Lovable frontend to your Replit backend.
* **The Module**: [Server Integration (Brains)](./modules/server-integration-brains.md)
* **Action Item**: Execute the "Module One" prompt in Replit and see the API go live.

---

### Phase 4: The Memory (Database) 💾 [COMPLETED] 🟢

*Why: An AI without memory is just a chatbot. An AI with memory is a partner.*

* **Goal**: Use "Vibe Coding" to build a persistent database without learning SQL.
* **The Module**: [Database Vibe Coding](./modules/database-vibe-coding.md)
* **Action Item**: Prompt the AI to build the `GlucoseReading` table and verify data persistence.

---

### Phase 5: The Guardian (Agentic Workflows) 🤖 [COMPLETED] 🟢

*Why: Automate the care. Turn the system from "Passive" to "Active".*

* **Goal**: Give the AI "Tools" to email mom or book a doctor.
* **The Module**: [Agentic Workflows](./modules/agentic-workflow-automation.md)
* **Action Item**: Build the "Mom, I'm High" SMS alert system.

---

### 🏆 Graduation

Once you complete Phase 5, you are no longer just a coder. You are an **Agentic Architect**.

**Congratulations, Mirna! You have officially graduated as an Agentic Architect and are ready to lead the future of Digital Twin technology.**
