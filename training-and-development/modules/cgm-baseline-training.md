# 🩸 Module: CGM Baseline Training

## Goal
Understand the fundamentals of Continuous Glucose Monitoring (CGM) and how daily life impacts glucose curves.

## Background
Continuous Glucose Monitoring (CGM) provides real-time data on glucose levels, allowing us to see trends, patterns, and the immediate impact of diet, exercise, and stress.

### Key Concepts
- **Time in Range (TIR)**: The percentage of time a person spends in their target glucose range (typically 70-180 mg/dL).
- **Hypoglycemia**: Low blood sugar.
- **Hyperglycemia**: High blood sugar.
- **Post-Prandial Spike**: The rise in glucose after eating.

## The Coffee Experiment ☕
To understand how sensitive the body is to inputs, simulate or perform the "Coffee Experiment":
1. **The Question**: Does coffee alone raise your glucose?
2. **The Test**: Drink coffee on an empty stomach and observe the CGM curve for 2 hours.
3. **The Variable**: Add milk or sugar. Compare the curves.

### Observation Guide
- Look for the "Peak" (highest point).
- Look for the "Trough" (lowest point).
- Note the "Velocity" (how fast it rises/falls).
