# 🩺 Module: CGM Baseline Training

## Mastering Your Glucose Curve (The Coffee Series & Beyond)

**Objective**: Execute high-signal "baseline experiments" to understand your personal glucose targets (Rise Speed, Peak Height, Recovery Time) and build your "State Engine" rules.

---

### ✅ Pre-Flight Checklist

*Ensure these conditions are met before **every** experiment to eliminate noise.*

- [ ] **Fasting**: At least 8-10 hours (water only).
- [ ] **Sleep**: You had a "normal" night's sleep (no major deprivation).
- [ ] **Health**: You are not sick or fighting an infection.
- [ ] **Activity**: No high-intensity exercise 2 hours before the test.
- [ ] **Time**: Start at the same time each morning (+/- 30 mins).

---

### ⏱️ The Universal Protocol (Standard Operating Procedure)

*Follow this exact timing for every "Day A / Day B" test.*

| Relative Time | Action | Notes |
|---------------|--------|-------|

### ☕ Phase 1: The Coffee Experiments

*Run these in the morning. Keep sleep and timing consistent.*

#### Experiment 1: The Control (Caffeine Sensitivity)

*Does caffeine alone spike your glucose?*
- **Hypothesis**: Black coffee should show a minor rise (<10mg/dL) due to cortisol, but no sharp spike.
- **Protocol**:
  - **Day 1A (Water Only)**: Drink 12oz of water at T=0.
  - **Day 1B (Black Coffee)**: Drink 12oz of black coffee at T=0.
- **Metric**: Compare the **Peak Height** of Day B vs Day A.

#### Experiment 2: Timing (The Breakfast Anchor)

*Should you caffeinate before or after food?*
- **Hypothesis**: Coffee *after* food blunts the cortisol response because there's already insulin active.
- **Protocol**:
  - **Day 2A (Pre-Game)**: Drink coffee at T=0. Eat breakfast at T+30.
  - **Day 2B (Post-Game)**: Eat breakfast at T=0. Drink coffee at T+30.
- **Metric**: Compare the **Area Under the Curve (Total Glucose)** for the 2-hour window.

#### Experiment 3: Composition (The Milk/Oat Variable)

*Are your add-ins sneaking in carbs?*
- **Hypothesis**: Dairy milk has lactose (sugar) and oat milk has maltose (spiky sugar). Both will likely spike higher than black coffee.
- **Protocol**:
  - **Day 3A (Black)**: 12oz Black Coffee.
  - **Day 3B (With Milk)**: 12oz Coffee + your standard amount of milk/creamer.
- **Metric**: Compare **Rise Speed** (how vertical is the arrow?).

#### Experiment 4: The Sweetener Test

*Is your "sugar-free" sweetener actually neutral?*
- **Hypothesis**: Liquid sugar/syrup causes a vertical spike. Artificial sweeteners *should* be flat, but some trigger an insulin response.
- **Protocol**:
  - **Day 4A (Unsweetened)**: Coffee with NO sweetener.
  - **Day 4B (Sweetened)**: Coffee with your usual sweetener.
- **Metric**: Compare **Peak Height**. If 4B is >20mg/dL higher than 4A, your sweetener is active.

#### Experiment 5: The Protein Buffer

*Can protein blunt the caffeine jitters and glucose rise?*
- **Hypothesis**: Protein stimulates glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) and slows gastric emptying.
- **Protocol**:
  - **Day 5A (Coffee Alone)**: Black coffee on an empty stomach.
  - **Day 5B (Protein First)**: Eat 2 hard-boiled eggs or a protein shake. Wait 15 mins. Drink coffee.
- **Metric**: Compare **Recovery Time**. 5B should return to baseline faster.

### 🥗 Phase 2: Food & Movement Levers

*Once you've mastered the coffee variable, test these powerful glucose levers.*

#### Experiment 6: The "Walk" Test (Post-Prandial Activity)

*The most powerful tool for blunting a post-meal spike.*
- **Hypothesis**: Contracting muscles suck up glucose without needing insulin. Walking should cut the peak by 20-30%.
- **Protocol**:
  - **Meal**: Eat the *exact same* carb-heavy lunch (e.g., a sandwich) on both days.
  - **Day 6A (Sit)**: Eat lunch. Sit at your desk for 2 hours.
  - **Day 6B (Walk)**: Eat lunch. Immediately go for a **15-minute brisk walk**.
- **Metric**: Compare **Peak Height**.

#### Experiment 7: Food Sequencing (The Veggie Starter)

*Fiber creates a mesh in your gut that slows sugar absorption.*
- **Hypothesis**: Eating veggies first delivers fiber to the stomach, slowing the absorption of the subsequent carbs.
- **Protocol**:
  - **Meal**: A plate with Rice/Bread (Carbs), Meat (Protein), and Salad/Broccoli (Fiber).
  - **Day 7A ( Chaos)**: Eat everything mixed together or carbs first.
  - **Day 7B (Sequenced)**: Eat **Veggies first**, then **Protein**, and save **Carbs for last**.
- **Metric**: Compare **Rise Speed** and **Peak Height**. 7B should be a flatter curve.

#### Experiment 8: The Protein Pre-Load

*The "Second Meal Effect" / Glucagon suppression.*
- **Hypothesis**: Protein signals the body to prepare for food, releasing a small amount of insulin early.
- **Protocol**:
  - **Meal**: A standard carb-heavy dinner (e.g., pasta).
  - **Day 8A (Control)**: Eat the pasta alone.
  - **Day 8B (Pre-Load)**: Eat **20g of protein** (shake, eggs, chicken) **30 minutes before** the pasta.
- **Metric**: Compare **Area Under the Curve**. The pre-load should significantly reduce the total impact.

### 📊 Interpretation Guide: Analyzing Your Data

#### 1. The "Spike" vs. The "Rise"

- **Rise**: A gentle elevation (< 20 mg/dL) that comes down slowly. Normal for black coffee due to cortisol.
- **Spike**: A sharp, rapid increase (> 30 mg/dL) followed by a crash. Often caused by sugar or refined carbs (milk/creamer).

#### 2. Recovery is Key

- **Healthy**: You are back to within 10-15% of your baseline by the 2-hour mark.
- **Delayed**: You are still elevated at 2 hours. This suggests insulin resistance or a high-fat/protein delay (like the "Pizza Effect").

#### 3. The "Dawn Phenomenon"

- If your glucose rises *before* you even drink anything, that's the Dawn Phenomenon (liver dumping sugar to wake you up). Note this in your "Baseline" log.
